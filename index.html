The story so far

Four chapters carry the whole team arc. Pick a hero below to see how each of them lived it.

2012

Assemble

Nick Fury’s long-shot Avengers Initiative becomes real when Loki steals the Tesseract and sends an alien army over New York.

2015

Ultron

Tony Stark and Bruce Banner try to build a global shield. They get Ultron, a machine that decides humanity itself is the threat.

2016

Civil War

After innocent lives are lost, the Sokovia Accords ask the team to answer to governments. Tony says yes and Steve says no, so the team splits.

2018–19

Infinity and Endgame

Thanos collects the Stones and erases half of all life. Five years later the survivors go back through time to undo it, at a heavy cost.
